Camper Crew

The camper drives itself. Mostly. You are the only crew on board: get to the campsite before the sun sets.

Road 1
How to play
  • Move with the stick (left thumb), WASD/arrows or a gamepad.
  • Grab picks up and puts down (Space, gamepad A).
  • Use throws, takes the wheel, patches and sprays (E, gamepad B/X). Throws aim themselves at the right spot.
  • Bends: sit at the wheel or the camper drifts off the road.
  • Fuel: take a can from the crate and throw it into the fuel filler.
  • Hungry passenger: fridge → stove → passenger. Don't let it burn.
  • Rain makes the roof leak: patch it with the kit. Puddles are slippery.
  • Potholes knock luggage off the shelf. Put it back.

Stars: ★ reach the campsite, ★ with the mood at half or more, ★ with 20 seconds of daylight to spare.

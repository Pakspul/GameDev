How to play

Tap a bolt to lift its top nuts, then tap another bolt to screw them on. Nuts only go onto an empty bolt or onto a nut of the same colour, and matching nuts travel together. Gather each colour on a bolt of its own.

Level types rotate as you climb: Mixed bolts of different lengths, Short 3-nut bolts and lots of them, Tall bolts with many nuts per colour, and Mystery nuts that stay grey until they reach the top.

Stars and washers. Finish within par for 3 stars. Using a hint or a spare bolt costs one star, and going far over par costs another. Every new star earns washers, and the daily puzzle pays extra for a streak. Spend washers on hints, spare bolts (each holds a single nut) and new boards and bolt finishes. New levels unlock as you clear them.

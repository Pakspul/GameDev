How to play
  • Tap an arrow. It slides off the board in the direction its head points.
  • If another arrow is in the way, it bounces back and you lose a heart. You have three per level.
  • You get one hint per level. It lights up an arrow that can leave, picking the one that frees the most others.
  • Clear a level without the hint for 3 stars, with the hint for 2.
  • Level numbers are fixed boards: Extra Hard level 23 is the same board for everyone, every time.
  • Pinch, scroll or use the zoom buttons on big boards. Drag to move around when zoomed in.
  • Boxes and Shapes: drag to turn the shape (arrow keys work too). Arrows can bend over edges, but each one leaves by sliding straight off the edge of the side its head is on. Check the hidden sides before you tap.
  • Shapes have inside corners. An arrow pointing into an inside corner hits a wall, so it can only leave the other way.
  • Curved sides count as one side. An arrow keeps sliding around the curve and only leaves at the next sharp edge.
  • Points: a 3-star clear pays the tier's full points, 2 stars pays 60%. The first clear of a level without losing a heart adds 25%. The daily puzzle pays 60 points plus up to 60 more for a streak. Spend them in the shop.

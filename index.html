Why are there no ads?

Most "free" mobile games are built to sell your attention: an ad after every level, timers you can skip for money, and tracking you never agreed to. These games are made the other way round. They are free because they are free: no ads, no data collection, no paid shortcuts, and no internet connection needed.

The code is open, so anyone can check that promise, learn from it, or build on it.

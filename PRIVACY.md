# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

This policy covers the Honest Arcade games (Jade Pavilion, Nuts & Bolts, Outbound, Unscrew, Graphite, Crystal Reels, Wallbreaker), both the Android app and the browser version.

**We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.**

- The games contain no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or SDKs.
- The games do not need an internet connection and make no network requests while you play.
- The app asks for no permissions.
- Your game progress and settings (levels completed, stars, in-game rewards, sound on or off; in Crystal Reels also your play-money balance, level and statistics; in Wallbreaker also your credits, purchases made with them and statistics) are saved **only on your own device**, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.
- There are no accounts and no sign-in.

**Children.** Because no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children either.

**Changes.** If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with a new date at the top.

**Contact.** Questions? Open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
